# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other channel used for this project.

## In short

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, including on experimental results;
- taking responsibility for mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not just for ourselves.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and any
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through GitHub, via the private contact on
the maintainer's profile ([@sc0210](https://github.com/sc0210)). Do not report incidents
in a public issue. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The
maintainer applies the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
